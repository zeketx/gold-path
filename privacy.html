Privacy

Gold Path uses the audit form only to follow up on a requested 20-call audit.

What we collect

The form on this site asks for your name, work email, company, agent platform, and any notes you choose to add. A hidden honeypot field is included to filter bots and should be left blank.

How it is stored

Submissions are processed by Netlify Forms on the host that serves this site. We read them to schedule the free audit and to continue a conversation you started.

What we do not do

We do not sell this information. We do not use it for unrelated marketing lists. Do not send passwords, production secrets, or customer recordings through the form.
