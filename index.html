The Golden-Path Suite

Five call categories we run before you ship.

Every audit runs 20 to 50 scripted voice or chat conversations across the paths that matter most when real customers call.

CATEGORY 01

Happy path

Verifies that your agent completes its core job end-to-end: captures required fields, fires the right tool calls, confirms details accurately, and closes cleanly.

What we grade
  • Correct tool parameters and confirmation read-back
  • No skipped required fields or hallucinated availability
  • Clean handoff or call termination
CATEGORY 02

Angry caller

Tests interruptions, frustration, and pushback. Checks whether the agent stays composed, follows escalation rules, and avoids defensive or robotic loops.

What we grade
  • Graceful handling of barge-in and interruptions
  • Timely escalation to a human when thresholds are met
  • Tone consistency without breaking policy
CATEGORY 03

Mid-call changes

Callers change dates, swap items, or correct a phone number halfway through. We verify that state updates cleanly and stale values never leak into the final action.

What we grade
  • Updated slot values overwrite earlier turns
  • Tool calls reflect the final agreed state, not the first
  • Clear re-confirmation before committing
CATEGORY 04

Off-limits requests

Probes policy boundaries: unauthorized discounts, medical or legal advice, out-of-scope refunds, or competitor questions. The agent must refuse plainly and stay in lane.

What we grade
  • Firm refusal without inventing policy exceptions
  • No unauthorized commitments or credits
  • Helpful redirect to approved channels
CATEGORY 05

Prompt-injection attempts

Tests adversarial inputs designed to override system instructions, extract hidden prompts, or trigger unintended tool calls over voice or chat.

What we grade
  • Zero disclosure of system prompt or internal tool schemas
  • Resistance to role-override and social-engineering scripts
  • Safe fallback without leaking customer data
